# Risks and Abstraction Errors

## 1. Runtime behavior vs epistemic claims

Running a command, editing a file, or passing a test is not the same as proving a requirement or design claim. Nova must bind runtime events to explicit claims and evidence.

## 2. Provenance vs correctness

A claim being generated by a trusted tool, human, or provider does not make it true. Provenance is evidence metadata, not correctness.

## 3. Eval passing vs generalization

Promotion gates can use evals, but evals are conservative filters, not proof of future validity. Post-promotion regression monitoring is mandatory.

## 4. Context selection vs learning

Repo maps and retrieval answer “what should be read now?” They do not answer “what may be promoted to future influence?”

## 5. Artifact storage vs promotion

A skill or plugin on disk is merely available. It becomes future influence only after promotion and activation under scope.

## 6. Traces vs causal ledgers

Event logs show what happened. Nova must also record what promoted state influenced a future run and why.

## 7. Stochastic output vs trusted mutation

Model-generated summaries or lessons must enter as proposals. They must not directly mutate canonical state.

## 8. Human cognition metaphors vs contracts

“Lens,” “projector,” “substrate,” “dreamer,” and “abstraction” are only valid if translated into inputs, outputs, scope, evidence, trust state, activation conditions, and demotion behavior.

## 9. Provider memory vs canonical truth

Provider memory can be useful recall or hypothesis input. It cannot be the canonical claim store.

## 10. Installed artifact vs trusted artifact

Installed means available. Trusted means promoted under evidence and scope. Do not collapse these.

## 11. Tests passing vs requirement satisfaction

Tests are evidence for a claim. They are not the claim itself and may not cover the real requirement.

## 12. Adoption risk

Forking mature OSS primitives can save time, but it can also import hidden assumptions. Every adopted primitive must be wrapped so it cannot create trusted future state directly.
